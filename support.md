# Basalt Fuel Support

Need a hand? Email **hereatbasalt@gmail.com** and tell us what happened. We read every message.

## Signing in

**I forgot my password.** On the Sign in screen, type your email and tap Forgot password? Check your email for the reset link. It can land in spam.

**I have a new phone.** Install Fuel, tap Already have an account? and sign in the same way as before. Your data comes back.

**I want to use a different account.** Fuel keeps one account on a phone. Go to Profile and tap Log out, then sign in to the other account.

## Your number

**Why did my number change?** It updates every Friday from your weigh-ins and your food log, so it keeps matching what your body is really doing. The Strategy page shows your number and how it's set.

**Is Fuel medical advice?** No. Fuel is a tracking tool. If you're pregnant, have a medical condition or have had an eating disorder, talk to your doctor before changing how you eat.

## Apple Health

Fuel can save the food you log and your weigh-ins to Apple Health. It doesn't read anything from Apple Health. To turn this off, open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Basalt Fuel.

## Community

**Someone is bothering me, or a post breaks the rules.** Use Report on the post or message. You can block someone from there too, which hides them everywhere. We read every report.

**I don't want people to find me.** Go to Profile, then Edit my card, and turn off Let people find me. You won't show up in search. Your friends and chats stay the same. To let someone you choose add you, tap Share my add card on the same page and send it to them.

## Your data

**Get a copy:** Profile, then Privacy & data, then Export your data.

**Delete your account:** Profile, then Privacy & data, then Delete account & data. If you can't get into the app, email us from your account's address and we'll do it for you.

Read our [privacy policy](privacy).
