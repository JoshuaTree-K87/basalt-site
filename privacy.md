# Basalt — Privacy Policy

**Effective: June 11, 2026**

Basalt ("the app") is a fitness application for tracking workouts, nutrition, and
personal goals. This policy describes what data Basalt handles and how.

## What we collect

- **Profile you provide**: name, sex, date of birth, height, weight, activity level,
  training background, and goals — used solely to compute your training and
  nutrition targets.
- **Activity you log**: workouts, sets and loads, cardio sessions, foods and meals,
  bodyweight, body measurements, water, and focus/goal progress.
- **Health data (optional)**: if you connect Apple Health, Basalt reads steps, heart
  rate, and energy burned to power automatic tracking, and writes your completed
  workouts back to Apple Health. **Health data is used only for these app features.
  It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with
  third parties.**
- **Social content (optional)**: a display name, friend connections, and messages or
  shared items you choose to send. Discoverability is controlled by you.
- **Account identifier**: Basalt creates an anonymous account identifier to back up
  your data. No email or password is required to use the app.

## How it's stored

Your data is stored on your device first, and backed up to our cloud database
(Google Firebase, in the United States) under your account identifier so it can be
restored. Transport is encrypted (HTTPS). Access is restricted by per-user security
rules — only your signed-in device can read your data; messages are readable only
by their participants.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, no ad networks, no trackers.
- No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties.
- No use of health data for any purpose other than the app's own features.

## Third-party services

- **Google Firebase** — authentication, database, and cloud functions (data
  processing/storage).
- **FatSecret** — food database search. Your food search terms (and barcode numbers
  you scan) are sent to our server, which queries FatSecret; no personal identity
  accompanies these queries.
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — subscription billing. We never see your
  payment details.

## Subscriptions

Basalt Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed by Apple or Google. Manage or
cancel anytime in your store account settings.

## Your controls

- **Export**: Settings → Privacy & data → Export my data.
- **Delete**: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all data — wipes the device and the
  cloud backup immediately.
- **Health access**: revocable anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health.
- **Discoverability/messaging**: toggle off anytime in Friends; block or report any
  user from their profile or a message.

## Children

Basalt is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data
from them.

## Changes

We'll update this page when the policy changes; material changes will be noted in
the app.
