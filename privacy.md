# Basalt Fuel Privacy Policy

**Effective: November 7, 2026**

Basalt Fuel ("Fuel") is an iPhone app for logging food, setting calorie and macro targets, and following your weight and body over time. It is made by Basalt ("we"). This page says what Fuel collects, why, who else handles it, and how to see it or delete it.

## The short version

- We use your data to run Fuel. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.
- Fuel has no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
- What you log is private to your account unless you choose to share it.
- You can export your data and delete your account inside the app, at any time.

## What Fuel collects

**Your account.** Fuel needs an account so your data can be backed up. You sign in with Apple, Google, or an email and password. We keep your email address and, if Apple or Google passes it on, your name. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only see Apple's relay address. Google Firebase handles passwords; we never see them.

**Your profile.** Your name, sex, birthday, height, weight, activity level and goals. Fuel uses these to set your calorie and macro targets. Your birthday also confirms you're old enough to use Fuel.

**What you log.** Foods, meals, recipes and saved foods; water; weigh-ins and body measurements; your Kitchen's stock, prep, plans, prices and budget; and your reminder times.

**Photos.** Your profile picture, meal and recipe photos, progress photos, and pictures you send in chats. Fuel only gets the photos you pick or take. It uses the camera to scan barcodes and to take progress photos. Your progress photos are backed up to storage that only your account can read.

**Social, if you use it.** Your friends and friend requests, the messages you send, your posts, replies and reactions in the feed, and the reports and blocks you make.

**Household, if you use it.** The items, recipes and plans you share with your household.

**Your device.** An address that lets us send notifications to your phone, and crash and performance reports. These reports include details such as your phone model, iOS version and app version, and what went wrong. They don't include your name, your email or what you log.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, Fuel saves the food you log and your weigh-ins to Apple Health. Fuel does not read anything from Apple Health. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and it is never sold.

## How we use it

We use your data to:

- run the app and back up your data, so it comes back when you sign in on a new phone
- work out your targets and update them from your log and your weigh-ins
- find foods, and deliver your messages and notifications
- keep the community safe, including reading reports and removing posts that break the rules
- fix crashes and keep the app working well
- answer you when you contact us

## Who can see what

- **Only you:** your food log, weigh-ins, measurements, progress photos, Kitchen and profile details, unless you choose to share them.
- **Anyone signed in to Fuel:** your public name and profile picture, which people can find by searching for your name, and your posts and replies in the feed. To stay out of search, turn off Let people find me in Profile, then Privacy & data. People who already know you through a chat, your friends list or the feed still see your name and picture.
- **Your friends, and people you've asked to be friends:** your card. It shows your name, your picture, a line you write, and the things you choose to show on it, such as your streak, your recipes, your goal or your recent weight. Your goal and weight stay off unless you turn them on.
- **People in a chat with you:** the messages and pictures you send there.
- **Your household:** what you share with it.
- **Us:** when someone reports a post or a message, we read it, including what was said.

## Who handles your data for us

We use these services to run Fuel. They handle data only to provide that service.

- **Google Firebase:** sign-in, database, file storage and server functions, in the United States.
- **Apple and Google:** sign-in, if you choose them. If you buy anything in Fuel, Apple handles the payment and we never see your payment details.
- **FatSecret, USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts:** food and nutrition data. Your food searches and scanned barcodes go to our server, which looks them up. Your name and account aren't sent to them.
- **KLIPY:** GIF search in chats. Your search words go straight from your phone to KLIPY, along with the usual connection details such as your IP address. Your name and account aren't sent.
- **Sentry:** crash and performance reports, as described above.
- **Expo:** delivers app updates and sends notifications through Apple. A notification's text, which can include the sender's name and the start of their message, passes through Expo and Apple on its way to your phone.

We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it or where it's needed to protect someone's safety.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. Crash and performance reports are removed automatically after a limited time. Copies in our providers' logs and backups can last a short time after you delete something.

## Deleting your account

Go to Profile, then Privacy & data, then Delete account & data.

This removes the data on your phone, your backup, your photos, your profile and card, your friends and friend requests, the messages you sent, your posts and replies, and your sign-in. If you signed in with Apple, Fuel also comes off your Apple ID.

Some things stay, because they belong to other people or keep people safe:

- messages other people sent you stay with them
- a shared household, and what's in it, stays for the people still in it
- reports you made stay as a safety record, without your name

If you can't get into the app, email us from your account's address and we'll delete your account for you.

## Your choices

- **Export:** Profile, then Privacy & data, then Export your data. You can get a spreadsheet or the full file.
- **Correct:** change your profile and what you've logged in the app.
- **Permissions:** turn off camera, photos and notifications in iPhone Settings, and Apple Health in the Health app.
- **Being found:** turn off Let people find me in Profile, then Privacy & data.
- **Block and report:** block someone or report a post or a message from the post or the chat.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. To do any of this, use the in-app tools above or email us. We won't treat you differently for asking. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you're in the EU or UK, we rely on our contract with you to run Fuel, on your consent for your health data and for Apple Health, and on our legitimate interests for safety and for fixing crashes. Your data is processed in the United States.

## Consumer health data

Under some US state laws, including those of Washington and Nevada, what you log about your food, weight and body counts as consumer health data. We collect it from you, use it only for the purposes above, and share it only with the services listed above and with the people you choose. We never sell it. You can see it, export it and delete it as described above, and you can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account.

## Children

Fuel is not for anyone under 13, and setup stops if your birthday shows you're under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

## Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules keep your account's data to your account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us if you think something is wrong.

## Changes

When this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date. If a change is important, we'll say so in the app.
